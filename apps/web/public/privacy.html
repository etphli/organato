Organato

Privacy statement

Your school data stays yours.

Organato is a private, single-owner school command centre. It has no advertising, third-party analytics, data resale, or AI training on your content.

What Organato accesses

Organato reads upcoming assignment data from the owner’s configured Canvas installation. It manages events only inside a dedicated app-created Google Calendar and uses Gmail send permission only to deliver reminders to recipients chosen by the owner.

Storage and protection

Private data is stored in the owner’s Cloudflare D1 database. Provider credentials are encrypted server-side and are never returned to the browser. Organato requests only the narrow Google permissions needed for its Calendar and email features.

Your controls

The owner can export a secret-free backup, archive and restore records, revoke active sessions, disconnect integrations, or permanently delete Organato data from Settings. Deleting Organato data does not delete the original records held by Canvas or Google.

Google access can also be revoked from Google Account permissions. Canvas access can be revoked by deleting the personal access token in Canvas.

Questions or deletion help

For privacy questions, deployment support, or help deleting data, contact the project owner through the Organato support tracker. Do not include school records, access tokens, or other secrets in a support request.
